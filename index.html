Operating Principles

i. information is fundamental

Most systemic problems — and most of their solutions — are information problems in disguise. Private information forms identity; public information forms civilisation. I design accordingly.

ii. asynchronous depth

Synchronous communication is a bottleneck. Build tools that allow deep, considered exchange across time. Reserve real-time for the relationships that have already earned it.

iii. abundance over scarcity

The best networks are positive-sum: the value to each participant should grow with the size of the whole. If a system requires someone to lose for someone else to win, it is the wrong system.

iv. keep calm & do kaam

Kaam, in Hindi, means work. The right pace is not the fastest one — it is the one that allows clear thinking, sustained effort, and a long enough horizon for compounding to do its job.
