The Bottleneck

Alignment must scale, and acceleration must compound. The bottleneck on both — especially for human minds — is the same one: communication.

Bend the rate at which minds can find, transmit and verify each other's signal — and you bend the timeline on every goal downstream.

That is why a communication product can sit underneath a mortality mission without it being a stretch. The lever is upstream of everything.

VERSE · Exponential Communication

VERSE is the lever I have chosen. A private, asynchronous network where language models handle the repetitive opening of every connection — so finite human attention can be spent only on the parts of a conversation that actually matter.

Dextra Founder

Acquired

Introbot Founder

An AI-powered networking assistant living inside WhatsApp. During the second wave of COVID-19 in India, it was rebuilt overnight into a resource-matching tool — connecting patients with verified medical supplies and hospital beds. Transitioned out; company still operating

The bottleneck-buster. The lever I expect to spend the rest of my life pulling.
