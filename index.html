Everything is information.

The world is a simulation. Matter, energy, thought, conversation — every one of them is a pattern of bits. Once that frame snaps into place, every other question downstream is answered by understanding and organizing data.

Only two things can understand and organize information: the human minds we have always had, and the artificial ones we are now building. These are the only two players on the board.
